Observations, estimates and validation limits

Both cameras share the same world coordinates. Models are not independently recentered or rescaled. Scan-to-model registration errors, differences in cloth and moving objects, and inferred hidden surfaces remain. Overall absolute accuracy has not been validated.

The shared cut plane clips both displays at the same height without changing the models. Upper and lower floors use the same multi-elevation scan; their registration is independent of the main floor. Intermediate, open-state and experimental variants are also available, but are not necessarily better than the latest candidates.
